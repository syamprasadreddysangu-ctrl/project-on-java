Freedom Fighters of India

Remembering the heroes who sacrificed their lives for our freedom 🇮🇳

Mahatma Gandhi

Father of the Nation

Mahatma Gandhi (1869–1948) was the leader of India's freedom movement against British rule. He followed the path of non-violence (Ahimsa) and truth (Satya). His major movements include the Non-Cooperation Movement, Dandi March, and Quit India Movement.

Bhagat Singh

Revolutionary Hero

Bhagat Singh (1907–1931) was a fearless revolutionary who fought against British oppression. He participated in the Central Legislative Assembly bombing and was executed at the age of 23. His bravery inspired millions of Indians.

Subhas Chandra Bose

Netaji

Subhas Chandra Bose (1897–1945) was a nationalist leader who formed the Indian National Army (INA). His famous slogan was “Give me blood, and I will give you freedom.” He believed in armed struggle to free India.

Rani Lakshmibai

Queen of Jhansi

Rani Lakshmibai (1828–1858) was one of the leading figures of the Indian Rebellion of 1857. She fought bravely against the British to protect her kingdom and became a symbol of courage and patriotism.
